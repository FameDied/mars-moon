   • Atmospheric Entry/EDL:   Landing at -7,150 m elevation provides Mars' densest atmosphere (1,155 Pa), doubling aerodynamic drag deceleration and saving ~12 metric tons of descent propellant (+45-60% payload gain), overwhelmingly offsetting the 11.4 km rover haul to lobate debris apron glacial ice.
   • Life Support Keep-Alive: Severe Martian dust storms (tau = 3.0) slash solar output by 93.3%; continuous habitability requires 2x 10 kWe Kilopower fission reactors (3,000 kg total) for weather-independent ECLSS life support.
   • Planetary Protection:    COSPAR Category IVb/c Special Region: Contains accessible ice requiring sterile drill barrier and a 1 km standoff to prevent biological forward contamination.
   • Analog Ground Testing:   Deploy chassis mobility and drilling validation to Danakil Depression (Dallol Hydrothermal Complex) (Ethiopia) (76.7% physical match), while qualifying cryogenic vacuum seals and volatile refrigeration systems at Bockfjorden Volcanic Complex (Svalbard) (Norway) / thermal-vac ground chambers at 35 K to achieve 80.5% combined system fidelity.
